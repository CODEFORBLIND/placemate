# PlaceMate

PlaceMate is a Multi-Agent Web platform designed to automate and streamline Placement-Cell activities for the Computer Science Department at Pune University.

The platform connects **Students** and **Placement-Cell (PC) Members** in one system.

### Students
- Maintain and manage their placement profile.
- Discover and apply for placement opportunities.
- Track their job applications.
- Upskill through an AI-powered tutor agent.

### Placement-Cell Members
- Manage placement drives and opportunities.
- Monitor student profiles and applications.
- Access centralized dashboards and placement activity.

The platform will use a **multi-agent architecture** to automate intelligent workflows such as tutoring, profile assistance, and placement-related tasks.
